title: "How to make a site like this"
---

This site was made using a project created by Jazz Weisman and Luke Weisman Miratrix, in honor of their dad, Joe Weisman. When Joe passed, Jazz and Luke built a memorial site for him, and then they configured it in such a way that other people could make their own memorial sites for their own loved ones easily and rapidly. You would do this by having Claude Code (an AI agent) help you walk through a series of relatively simple steps. The sites are easily customizable, and you do not need to be a major web developer to do this.

To read more about Joe, and read instructions for how to set up your own memorial site, go to [joeweisman.org](https://joeweisman.org). In particular, the site has [an overview of the process of making the site](https://joeweisman.org/how-to-make-this). You can also go directly to the GitHub repository at [github.com/jazzlw/joeweisman](https://github.com/jazzlw/joeweisman) for further information.
